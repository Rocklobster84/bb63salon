About

Established in 2010, BB 63 Salon has been a trusted staple in Fort Worth, Texas for over a decade. Our journey began on Blue Bonnet Circle near TCU and, as of October 2024, we are excited to announce our new home at 2901 Alta Mere Dr., Suite 300, Fort Worth, Texas 76116.

At BB 63 Salon, you will find some of the best hairstylists and aestheticians in town dedicated to ensuring every guest feels beautiful inside and out. We are committed to providing a serene and inviting atmosphere for all your beauty services. Each team member takes great pride in delivering exceptional service tailored to your individual needs.

Explore our full range of services and consider scheduling an aesthetic treatment alongside your hair service for a complete pampering experience. We look forward to welcoming you to BB 63 Salon soon!

Interested in renting a chair or suite at our salon? Find out more information here: Booth or Suite Rental Info.

Our Location

Our new location on Alta Mere Drive, just South of I-30, completes the whole package in the heart of central Fort Worth, where great dining, shopping, and entertainment can be found a stone's throw away. So, whether you are headed off to a special night in the Cultural District nearby or just want to freshen up your everyday look, BB 63 Salon is your one-stop-shop to celebrate and refresh your own unique beauty and style.
